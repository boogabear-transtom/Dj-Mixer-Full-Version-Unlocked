## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Automatic Mixing Options** — Simplifies the mixing process by automatically transitioning between tracks, allowing DJs to focus on creativity rather than technicalities.
- **Tempo and Pitch Detection** — Automatically analyzes tracks to ensure they are aligned in tempo and pitch, facilitating smoother mixes.
- **Loop Creation and CUE Points** — Empower users to set loops and CUE points easily, enhancing control over the mixing process.
- **BPM Counter** — Provides real-time beats per minute (BPM) analysis, crucial for maintaining the correct tempo during live performances.
- **VU Meter** — Monitors audio levels visually, helping DJs maintain balanced sound throughout their sets.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Support for Low Latency ASIO Drivers** — Optimizes performance on compatible systems, ensuring a responsive mixing experience with minimal lag.
- **Six Effects of Various Types** — Offers a variety of audio effects to enhance mixes, including echo, reverb, and flanger, providing creative flexibility.
- **Drag-and-Drop Interface** — Users can easily load music files into the application by dragging and dropping, making it convenient to manage playlists.
- **Automatic Gain Leveling** — Adjusts the volume levels of tracks automatically, ensuring a consistent sound output without manual adjustments.
